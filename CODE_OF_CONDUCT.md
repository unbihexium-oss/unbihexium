## Abstract

This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behaviour expected from everyone who takes part in the Unbihexium project, how breaches can be reported and how reports are handled. It is addressed to contributors, maintainers, users and anyone else who interacts with the project. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 [1], whose pledge, standards and enforcement guidelines it keeps; the adaptations describe the project's reporting channel, its current single-maintainer structure and the escalation path to GitHub that follows from it.

## 1. Pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## 2. Standards

### 2.1 Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community.

In the technical work of the project this also means basing arguments on evidence, reviewing the change rather than the person, and stating honestly what a piece of software, model or result can and cannot do.

### 2.2 Unacceptable behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- the use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or e-mail address, without their explicit permission;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 3. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces of the project: the repository `unbihexium-oss/unbihexium` and the other repositories of the `unbihexium-oss` organisation on GitHub, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commit messages and security advisories, as well as e-mail exchanged with the maintainers about the project. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces, for example when using an official e-mail address, posting through an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## 4. Responsibilities

The maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) are the community leaders responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behaviour, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## 5. Reporting

### 5.1 Reporting channel

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement by e-mail to <yunus.z.imanov@helsinki.fi>. A report should, where possible, contain:

- the names or account names of the people involved;
- a description of what happened, with the date and the place (for example the link to the issue or pull request);
- links to or copies of the relevant content, since content may later be edited or deleted;
- whether the reporter wishes to remain anonymous towards the other people involved.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct matters; report them as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

### 5.2 Reports concerning a maintainer

The project currently has a single maintainer (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)), who is also the recipient of reports. If a report concerns that maintainer, or if a reporter is not comfortable reporting to the project, the reporter can use GitHub's own reporting tools: content and accounts can be reported to GitHub, which enforces the GitHub Community Guidelines [2] and the GitHub Acceptable Use Policies [3] on all of its sites. When further maintainers are added, reports concerning one maintainer will be handled by the others, and that maintainer will not take part in the decision.

## 6. Handling of reports

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident. In particular:

- reports are acknowledged and reviewed as promptly as the capacity of the project allows; the project does not guarantee a fixed response time;
- the identity of the reporter and the content of the report are not disclosed beyond the people handling it without the reporter's consent, unless disclosure is required by law;
- the people concerned are given the opportunity to present their view before a decision is taken, unless this would put the reporter or others at risk;
- decisions are communicated to the people concerned, and the reasons are given where appropriate;
- personal data in reports is used only to handle the report; the project's privacy notice is [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

Reporters and others who take part in handling a report in good faith are protected from retaliation. Retaliation is itself a breach of this Code of Conduct.

## 7. Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct.

### 7.1 Correction

**Community impact:** use of inappropriate language or other behaviour deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence:** a private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 7.2 Warning

**Community impact:** a violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 7.3 Temporary ban

**Community impact:** a serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behaviour.

**Consequence:** a temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 7.4 Permanent ban

**Community impact:** demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behaviour, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** a permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

On GitHub, bans are implemented by blocking the account from the `unbihexium-oss` organisation. A person subject to a decision may ask for it to be reconsidered by writing to the reporting address; a reconsideration is decided, where possible, by a maintainer who did not take the original decision.
